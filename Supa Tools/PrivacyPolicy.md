# Supa Tools Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

**Supa Tools is built offline-first.** Most features — PDF tools, image tools, QR/barcode generation, and business card creation — run on your device. Your files, scans, and creations are not uploaded to our servers for those tools.

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Supa Tools**, an offline-friendly toolkit for PDF tools, image editing, QR & barcode scanning/generation, business cards, and related utilities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices. By using Supa Tools, you agree to this Policy.

## 2. About Supa Tools

Many features process data **on your device**, often without an internet connection:

- **PDF tools:** merge, split, compress, PDF ↔ image conversion  
- **Image tools:** compress, convert, resize, crop, background removal (where supported)  
- **QR & barcode:** live scan, scan from image, generate QR and barcodes  
- **Business cards & social QR:** design and export cards / profile QR links  

File conversions, edits, and generation for these tools are intended to happen locally. Content is not sent to our servers as part of those local workflows.

## 3. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only what is needed:

| Category | What happens |
|----------|----------------|
| **Your files & content** | PDFs, images, scan data, generated codes, and cards are processed/stored on your device unless you choose to share or send them elsewhere. |
| **Camera** | Used for live scanning/capture in the app. Frames are processed on-device for that purpose and are not uploaded by us for storage. |
| **Photos / files** | Only the items you pick via the system picker are accessed for the action you request. |
| **Device storage** | Saving writes files to locations you choose (e.g. Downloads / app folders) on the device. |
| **Optional feedback** | If you submit Contact & Feedback, we receive the name, email, and message you send (and may include app version / platform). |
| **Advertising** | If ads are shown (e.g. Google AdMob), partners may collect device identifiers and usage data per their policies. |
| **Diagnostics** | Basic crash or performance data may be collected to improve stability. |

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use the content of your files for advertising or training.

## 4. How We Use Information

- Provide the features you request (scan, convert, edit, generate, save, feedback)  
- Show ads where applicable, via advertising partners  
- Diagnose crashes and improve the app  
- Respond to support or feedback you send us  

## 5. Third-Party Services

Examples of third parties that may process limited data (not your local file contents for offline tools):

- **Google AdMob** — [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
- **Google Play / App Store** — install, updates, and platform analytics under their policies  
- **Feedback backend** (if used) — only the information you submit in the feedback form  

Each provider is governed by its own policy.

## 6. Data Security

On-device processing relies on your device security (lock screen, OS sandbox). Transmitted data (e.g. ads, feedback) uses standard HTTPS where applicable. No method is 100% secure.

## 7. Permissions

Permissions are requested when needed for a feature, for example:

- **Camera** — QR/barcode scanning and capture  
- **Microphone** — only if a feature needs it  
- **Photos / files / storage** — pick and save files you choose  

Where possible we use system pickers instead of broad library access.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may be able to:

- Opt out of personalized ads (device / Google settings)  
- Request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold (e.g. feedback emails)  
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent  

Local files remain under your control on the device; delete them in the app or from device storage.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Supa Tools is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have such data.

## 10. International Users

If you use the app from outside the country where our support email is based, you understand that limited data you send us (e.g. feedback) may be processed in the countries where we or our providers operate.

## 11. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
